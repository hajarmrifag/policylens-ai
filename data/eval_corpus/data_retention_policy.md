# Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods
Financial records are retained for 7 years. Human resources records are retained for 5 years after the end of employment. System access logs are retained for 12 months.

## Customer data
Customer personal data must be deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request. Backups containing deleted customer data are purged within 90 days.

## Disposal
Paper records containing personal data must be shredded. Electronic media must be securely wiped before disposal or reuse.
