# Information Security Policy (current, version 3.1)

## Authentication
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for all corporate accounts. Passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Accounts are locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts and can only be unlocked by the IT service desk.

## Devices and software
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Critical security patches must be installed within 7 days of release. The use of personal USB storage devices on company equipment is prohibited.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents, including lost devices and phishing emails, must be reported to the security team within 24 hours of discovery.
