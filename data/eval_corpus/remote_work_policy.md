# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility and schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with the approval of their line manager. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the Human Resources department.

## Security when working remotely
A company VPN must be used whenever an employee connects to internal systems from a public or shared network. Company data must not be stored on personal devices.

## Equipment
Employees receive a one-time home office equipment allowance of 500 USD. Equipment purchased with the allowance remains company property.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is limited to 30 days per calendar year and requires prior approval from Human Resources.
